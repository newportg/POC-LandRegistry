# About

The land registry, officially [HM Land Registry](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/land-registry), is the government department responsible for registering the ownership of land and property in England and Wales. It provides a public, guaranteed record of who owns what, which includes title registers, title plans, and details of mortgages, covenants, and other rights associated with a property. This process establishes government-guaranteed titles, protects against fraud, and offers clear proof of ownership and boundaries. 

Key Functions

* **[Property Registration](https://www.gov.uk/registering-land-or-property-with-land-registry):**
    
    HM Land Registry registers the legal ownership of land and property, including information on the owner, the property's boundaries, and details of any mortgages or other rights attached to it. 

* **[Government Guaranteed Title](#Government-Guaranteed-Title):**

    The Land Register provides title deeds that are guaranteed by the government, giving property owners certainty and legal proof of ownership. 

* **Public Access:**
    
    The register is open to the public, allowing people to search for information about properties, such as who owns them, the last sale price, and any registered mortgages or covenants. 

*  **[Fraud Prevention](https://www.gov.uk/protect-land-property-from-fraud):**
    
    By providing a clear and accessible record of ownership, the Land Registry helps protect against property and mortgage fraud. 

* **[HM Land Registry - Property Alert](https://propertyalert.landregistry.gov.uk/):**
    
    A free service is available to monitor properties for official applications and searches. 
    

## Benefits of Registration

*   **Proof of Ownership:**
    A registered title serves as definitive proof of who owns a property. 
*   **Protection from Fraud:**
    The system helps to protect owners from attempts to illegally claim their property. 
*   **Facilitates Transactions:**
    A guaranteed title simplifies the process of selling or gifting a property. 
*   **Clarity and Certainty:**
    It provides a clear understanding of property ownership, boundaries, and any restrictions or rights affecting the land.

## Government Guaranteed Title

A "government-guaranteed title" typically refers to Title Absolute in England and Wales, which is the most secure type of property ownership, guaranteeing the owner has an undisputed right to the property. While not directly "guaranteed" by the government, [HM Land Registry](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/land-registry) registers this title, making it a legally recognized and robust form of ownership that provides the highest level of certainty to the owner. 

Key Aspects

* **Title Absolute:**
    This is the highest class of title, confirming an owner's absolute and undisputed right to the property. 
    
* **[HM Land Registry](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/land-registry):**
    The Land Registry is responsible for registering these titles, providing a clear record of ownership and property rights. 
    
* **Purpose:**
    It serves as the definitive legal proof of ownership, giving the owner confidence in their right to the land or property. 
    
* **How to Get it:**
    If you have possessory or qualified title, you can upgrade to Title Absolute by meeting specific criteria, such as demonstrating a long period of ownership and occupation of the land. 
    
* **Other Title Types:**
    Other title types include Qualified Title and Possessory Title, which are less secure but can often be upgraded to Title Absolute over time through the Land Registry process. 
    

In essence, a Title Absolute is the closest concept to a "government-guaranteed title" in the UK, providing the ultimate assurance of property ownership through official registration.
